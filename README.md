# MuscleBearFoods

Static food index used by the Muscle Bear app. Served by GitHub Pages at
`https://ilieorthogonally.github.io/MuscleBearFoods/v1/`.

## Data source and license

All product data comes from **USDA FoodData Central — Branded Foods** (release 2026-10-29),
published by the U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service.
FoodData Central data is in the **public domain** (CC0 1.0 Universal).

Suggested citation: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service.
FoodData Central, 2026. fdc.nal.usda.gov.

This repository contains no other data. Product names and brand names are as reported to USDA
by manufacturers; trademarks belong to their owners and are used only to identify products.

## Layout (version 1)

- `v1/meta.json` — index version, bucket counts, build stats
- `v1/w/NNN.json` — brand finder: word → brands (each brand listed under its rarest word)
- `v1/b/<brand>.json` or `v1/b/<brand>/NN.json` — a brand's products (large brands split by word)
- `v1/u/NNN.json` — barcode lookup by the last 3 digits of the 13-digit code

Product record: `[code, name, kcal, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar, sat_fat, sodium_mg,
serving_g, serving_label, liquid, flagged, brand?]`, nutrients per 100 g.

Generated by `musclebear branded-index` (Muscle Bear pipeline). Do not edit by hand.
